# G0 Privacy, Security, Measurement, and Operations Risk Register

## Purpose and decision boundary

This is the live row-level risk register for the end-to-end usage-monitor program. It converts the summary risks in the [complete goal](./2026-07-24-end-to-end-multi-user-usage-monitor-goal.md) into controls, detection evidence, tests or drills, ownership, and explicit residual decisions.

An agent may implement and audit a control, but only Adam Allcock may accept a privacy or security residual, authorize external participants, authorize transmission, or authorize publication. `implemented` means the current source tree contains the stated control; it does not mean its residual is accepted or that a later release gate is open.

Review this register whenever a telemetry field, source directory, identity relation, transport, retention period, processor, public filter, supported platform, or incident path changes. Every G0/G1 receipt must cite the reviewed revision. Every G3/G4/G9/G10/G11 approval must resolve all applicable `blocking` rows and record the accepted lower-severity residuals in its dated gate receipt.

## Status vocabulary

- `implemented`: control and proportionate local evidence exist in the current tree.
- `partial`: some controls exist, but the stated test, drill, packaging proof, or operator decision is missing.
- `planned`: the risky capability is not authorized or implemented; its future gate owns the control.
- `blocking`: applicable now and insufficiently controlled; the named gate cannot pass.
- `accepted`: reserved for a dated human decision. No row is accepted by this document alone.
