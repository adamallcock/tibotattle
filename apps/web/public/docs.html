TiboTattle, from download to dashboard.

TiboTattle is a local-first Mac app for understanding personal Codex usage. It estimates your seven-day allowance in API-equivalent terms, keeps its reset history, and shows uncertainty instead of filling gaps with guesses.

Download security

How TiboTattle protects every macOS release

Every published DMG must come from an exact tagged source checkout and pass Developer ID signing, Apple notarization, stapling, Gatekeeper assessment, and checksum verification before the download appears on the website.

  • Open source Review the app and its release tooling in the public repository.
  • Clean tagged build The release tooling refuses a signed release from a dirty checkout or a commit that does not match its exact release tag.
  • Developer ID signed and Apple notarized macOS can verify the publisher, hardened signature, notarization ticket, and Gatekeeper result.
  • Exact bytes published The download page and Homebrew pin the release DMG to its SHA-256 checksum.

Verify your download

Compare the first command's output with the SHA-256 shown beside the Download button. Replace /path/to/TiboTattle.dmg with the DMG you downloaded.

  1. Check the downloaded bytes. shasum -a 256 "/path/to/TiboTattle.dmg"
  2. Ask Gatekeeper to assess the DMG before opening it. spctl --assess --type open --context context:primary-signature --verbose=4 "/path/to/TiboTattle.dmg"
  3. Ask Gatekeeper to assess the installed app. spctl --assess --type execute --verbose=4 /Applications/TiboTattle.app
  4. Inspect and verify the complete app signature. codesign -dv --verbose=4 /Applications/TiboTattle.app codesign --verify --deep --strict --verbose=2 /Applications/TiboTattle.app

What these checks establish

The checksum confirms that your copy matches the published DMG. The Apple checks confirm its Developer ID signature and notarization. TiboTattle's release gate separately requires a clean, exactly tagged source checkout.

A public cryptographic source-to-binary build attestation is the remaining supply-chain hardening step. It will be added to this guide when the release pipeline publishes one.

Start on your Mac

Check the public home page for download availability. When a signed Mac release is published, move TiboTattle to Applications and open it. The app hosts its private dashboard in its own window; this public website never scans your Codex files or accepts uploads.

Read your Codex week

  • Estimated API-equivalent value of the observed seven-day allowance.
  • Seven-day history across quota-reset observations.
  • Allowance metadata and uncertainty when the evidence supports it.

Local by default

Personal scans, pricing, and dashboard history stay on your Mac. Sign-in and community contribution are optional and are not needed to inspect your local usage.

Community evidence

The public site is read-only and does not enroll contributors. It may show a delayed community activity snapshot only after aggregate evidence passes privacy, support, and quality checks. Activity totals alone are never presented as an allowance.
