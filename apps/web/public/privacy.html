Privacy overview

Your dashboard belongs on your Mac.

TiboTattle separates personal analysis from public community evidence. Local usage remains useful without an account or contribution.

Public website

This website cannot read local Codex files. It serves product information, current download availability, and a reviewed aggregate community snapshot when one is published. It does not enroll contributors or accept uploads.

Mac app

TiboTattle reads content-free usage and quota metadata locally. Personal scans, API-equivalent pricing, and dashboard history stay on the Mac that calculated them.

Optional contribution

Nothing is contributed unless you review and opt in. The public website has no enrollment or upload control. Any contribution starts from an explicit app flow and contains bounded, content-free observations rather than prompts, responses, file contents, or personal dashboard history.

Community publication

Public figures are delayed, capped per eligible contribution account, rounded, and withheld unless enough eligible provider accounts pass privacy and quality review. Failed or weak evidence stays unavailable.
