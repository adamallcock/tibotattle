Local-first allowance monitoring

Your Codex allowance, measured on your own Mac.

TiboTattle reads content-free Codex metadata locally and shows what your allowance is actually doing. The dashboard lives inside the Mac app, because only the app can read your local files. This site has two jobs: hand you the app, and publish what the community has chosen to share.

Get the app

Install TiboTattle for Mac

A website cannot read Codex files, and TiboTattle does not ask it to. The Mac app runs a private loopback companion, reads metadata locally, and shows the full dashboard inside its own window. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly choose to contribute.

How this snapshot is produced

Community values use a fixed delay, independent per-cell support, participant-level clipping, and coarse rounding. A sealed revision is never rewritten; privacy-affecting deletion withdraws it and schedules a replacement revision without the deleted source.
