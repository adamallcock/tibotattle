TiboTattle application icon provenance

Source: Original artwork commissioned for this project on 2026-08-01. The mark
(a cream bird raising a wing to its beak as if whispering, on a deep green
rounded-square plate with an amber beak) was produced with an image-generation
model from the repository's own written design brief at
docs/design/2026-08-01-tibotattle-icon-brief.md, then prepared for macOS in
this repository: the generated background was replaced with real transparency,
the plate was masked to the standard squircle, a drop shadow was applied, and
the artwork was rendered to every required icon size. It incorporates no
third-party artwork, no typeface, no photograph, and no person's likeness.

Rights: Created as an original work for this repository. All rights held by
Adam Allcock, who reviewed the rendered artwork at full size and at 16- and
32-pixel sizes and approved it for distribution with the v0.1.0 release on
2026-08-01.
