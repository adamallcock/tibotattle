# TiboTattle 0.1.15

Finishes the fix 0.1.14 started. The charts stopped blanking; the usage figures
did not. Now they do too.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. Updating keeps your local data,
your Keychain items, and anything you have already contributed.

## Your usage figures stop disappearing

0.1.14 stopped the timeline and allowance-history charts emptying and refilling
on every refresh. It did not cover the usage totals or the usage timelines, so
on 0.1.14 those kept flickering — the charts held still while the numbers next
to them dropped out and came back.

The cause was the same one, in a place the first fix did not reach. Each refresh
loads the dashboard twice: a quick pass that surfaces your quota and pace as
soon as they are known, then the full pass once the whole history projection
finishes. The quick pass cannot rebuild your usage history, and it was
publishing zeroes rather than leaving the figures you already had in place.

The quick pass now updates only what it actually knows. Your quota and pace
still arrive immediately; the usage figures and both usage timelines stay put
until real replacements arrive.

If you were watching your usage flicker on 0.1.14, that is what this release is
for.

## Known limitation

Unchanged from 0.1.14: hours when your weekly allowance is exhausted and you
keep working on purchased credits are still excluded from the cost view. The
work is real and the spend is real; the comparison it is excluded from is quota
movement, which cannot move once the allowance reads 100%. Surfacing that spend
properly is planned.
