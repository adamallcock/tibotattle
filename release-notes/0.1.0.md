# TiboTattle v0.1.0

First public release. TiboTattle is a local-first macOS monitor for OpenAI
Codex usage: it reads your local Codex session metadata, prices your usage at
API-equivalent rates, tracks your weekly allowance in real time from the
provider's own quota telemetry, and — optionally, with one sign-in and one
approval — contributes anonymous, content-free usage aggregates to a community
estimate.

## What it does

- **Allowance tracking** — live weekly quota position, reset schedule, and
  pace, straight from provider telemetry on your machine.
- **Usage and costs** — every session, model, and speed priced at
  API-equivalent rates with historical price epochs respected.
- **Trends** — where observed quota movement and priced usage agree and
  where they diverge, stated honestly (single blended-rate expected line;
  per-model line planned).
- **Community (optional)** — anonymous daily aggregates. Sign in once
  (Google/Apple), approve once; the service keeps an irreversible hash, never
  your email. Full deletion control from the app.

## Privacy

Prompts, responses, code, paths, and account identifiers never leave your
Mac. Contribution uploads carry content-free token counts, quota
observations, and coarse session dimensions only — reviewable in the app
before anything is sent. No account required for local use.

## Requirements

macOS 14+ (Apple silicon), OpenAI Codex CLI/desktop with local sessions.
Signed and notarized; updates via Sparkle.
