# TiboTattle 0.1.16

Your charts and cost figures stay put. This release ends the blanking that
0.1.14 and 0.1.15 each fixed a part of.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. Updating keeps your local data,
your Keychain items, and anything you have already contributed.

## The dashboard stops emptying and refilling

Every few minutes, as the app refreshed, the Trends chart could flip to
"Not comparable yet", the Usage and costs page could drop to $0.00 and zero
tokens, and both would come back a minute or two later. If a background
recalculation fell behind, the empty state could stick around for much longer.
It looked exactly like your history vanishing. It never was: the data was
present and fully indexed the whole time.

The cause was one rule applied too broadly. While the app re-indexes new
activity, its freshly computed figures briefly do not line up with the new
index, and during that window the dashboard was being handed publications with
those sections blanked "to be safe" — and treated every publication as
authoritative, letting an empty one replace a good one. Three separate
surfaces went dark this way: the timeline charts, the capacity figure the
Trends comparison keys on, and the cost accounting itself.

Now a publication only replaces what it can vouch for. During a
recalculation window the app keeps showing the figures it already verified,
with the existing "history is still advancing" notice telling you a
recalculation is underway — and the moment the recalculation lands, the fresh
figures take over. A genuine change (say, clearing your history) still shows
immediately: an authoritative empty state always wins.

Honesty is preserved throughout: the app's own status fields — whether its
accounting matches the current index — always reflect the live state, never
the retained figures' state.

## Validated before release, on real data

This release was soaked on the internal dogfood channel against a 129 GB,
593,000-event real corpus before anything shipped: three monitored refresh cycles
with a recalculation window crossing each one — charts and cost figures
holding through every window, with the previous build blanking under identical
conditions minutes earlier as the control.

## Known limitation

On a fresh app launch, the dashboard may show loading states until the first
complete refresh finishes — retained figures live in memory, so a relaunch
starts without them. Serving the previous figures across launches is planned.

Hours when your weekly allowance is exhausted and you keep working on
purchased credits remain excluded from the cost view; surfacing that spend
properly is still planned.
