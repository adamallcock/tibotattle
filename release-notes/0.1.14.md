# TiboTattle 0.1.14

A focused fix for charts that kept emptying and refilling on 0.1.13.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. Updating keeps your local data,
your Keychain items, and anything you have already contributed.

## Your charts stop blanking

On 0.1.13 the timeline and allowance-history charts could empty out and refill
repeatedly — "No 3-hour series loaded", "No weekly estimates loaded" — and it
looked exactly like the app had lost your history. It had not. Every event was
still indexed the whole time.

Each refresh loads the dashboard twice: once as soon as the fast-moving figures
are known, so your quota and pace update straight away, and again when the full
history projection finishes. That first quick pass does not carry the heavy
charts, and it was publishing them as empty rather than leaving the ones you
already had in place. So the charts blanked once per refresh cycle and came
back at the end of it.

The quick pass now updates what it actually knows and leaves the rest alone.
You still get quota and pace immediately; the charts stay put until real
replacements arrive.

## Usage accounting on very large histories

- The rebuild's memory bound could be bypassed. A quota window whose reset time
  could not be read took a slice running from the very beginning of your
  history rather than from its own start, so a single such window pulled the
  entire corpus into one pass. It is now bounded to its own window, and one
  intermediate structure that stayed in memory for the whole pass is released
  as soon as it is finished with.
- When a rebuild does defer for memory, it now records what it measured rather
  than only that it happened. Deferrals had been effectively undiagnosable; a
  deferral now carries the memory it started from, the memory it reached, and
  the limit it crossed, so a report can be acted on instead of guessed at.

## Known limitation

Hours when your weekly allowance is exhausted and you keep working on purchased
credits are still excluded from the cost view. The work is real and the spend
is real; the comparison it is excluded from is quota movement, which cannot
move once the allowance reads 100%. Surfacing that spend properly is planned.
